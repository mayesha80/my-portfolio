Image Reconstruction from fMRI Signals

Built a deep latent-space model that reconstructs natural images directly from high-dimensional fMRI brain activity, with residual encoders and U-Net refinement mapping neural signals into Stable Diffusion's latent space.

  • Stable Diffusion
  • U-Net
